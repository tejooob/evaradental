Meet your doctor

Dr. Kshitija P Pervi

B.D.S (MUHS) · Registered Dental Practitioner, Reg No. A-44613

A graduate of the Maharashtra University of Health Sciences, Dr. Pervi founded Evara with a simple conviction: patients take better care of their teeth when they actually understand them. Her consultations are unhurried, bilingual in Marathi and English, and always begin with listening.

From a child's first check-up to full-mouth rehabilitation, she treats every case personally, and refers honestly when a specialist would serve you better.

Treatments provided

Complete dental care under one roof, from routine cleaning to full smile makeovers. Every procedure begins with a clear explanation and a written estimate.

Root Canal Treatment

Single-visit and multi-visit RCT that saves your natural tooth and ends the toothache, with rotary instruments for a faster, gentler procedure.

Dental Implants

A permanent, natural-feeling replacement for missing teeth, planned digitally and restored with precision-matched crowns.

Braces & Clear Aligners

Traditional braces and nearly invisible aligners to straighten teeth at any age, with regular reviews at every stage.

Teeth Whitening

Professional in-clinic whitening that lifts years of stains in a single sitting, safely and without sensitivity.

Crowns & Bridges

Ceramic and zirconia crowns matched to the exact shade of your natural teeth, restoring both strength and appearance.

Extractions & Wisdom Teeth

Painless removal of damaged and impacted teeth under local anaesthesia, with careful aftercare instructions.

Kids' Dentistry

Gentle first check-ups, cavity care and habit counselling that make the dentist a place children don't mind visiting.

Scaling & Polishing

Deep cleaning that removes tartar and stains, keeps gums healthy and freshens your breath. Recommended every six months.

Dentures

Comfortable complete and partial dentures, custom-fitted so you can eat and speak with confidence again.

Smile Design

Veneers, contouring and whitening combined into one plan, previewed with you before any work begins.
