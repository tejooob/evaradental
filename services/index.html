Treatments explained in full

The four we are asked about most often. Each has its own page: what the treatment is for, what happens at the appointment, what it feels like and what to do afterwards.

Teeth Cleaning

Scaling and polishing removes the hardened tartar a toothbrush cannot shift — the usual cause of bleeding gums and lasting bad breath. Normally one visit of 30 to 45 minutes.

Read the full guide →

Teeth Filling

A tooth-coloured composite filling repairs a cavity before it reaches the nerve, and is matched to the shade of your own tooth. Almost always finished in a single visit.

Read the full guide →

Root Canal Treatment

Saves a tooth whose nerve has become infected, so you keep your own tooth instead of losing it. One or two appointments, followed by a crown to protect it.

Read the full guide →

Implant Dentistry

Replaces a missing tooth with a titanium post in the jaw and a crown on top, without cutting down the healthy teeth on either side.

Read the full guide →

Also treated at Evara

Cleaning, fillings, root canals and implants have pages of their own above. Everything else we do is listed here.

Gums & Periodontal Care

Gums that bleed when you brush are not normal, and they are treatable at almost every stage.

  • Gum disease evaluation
  • Scaling & root planing
  • Treatment of bleeding gums
  • Gum infection management
  • Periodontal maintenance

Crowns, Bridges & Rebuilding

For teeth too broken down for a filling, and for gaps where an implant is not the right answer.

  • Dental crowns & caps
  • Bridges

Teeth Removal & Minor Surgery

Orthodontics & Smile Correction

How treatment works here

The same four things are true whichever treatment brings you in.

The cost, in writing

After the examination you get the diagnosis, the options and what each one costs — before anything begins. Nothing is added mid-procedure without asking you first.

One dentist, start to finish

Dr. Pervi treats every patient personally, so you are not handed between clinicians halfway through a course of treatment.

Sterilisation you can see

Instruments are autoclaved and laid out sealed in front of you. Disposables are fresh for every appointment.

You see your own X-ray

Digital X-rays and 3D oral scans go on the screen next to you, and the findings are explained before anything is decided.

Before your first visit

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Booking ahead is best, because appointments are unhurried and the slot is held for one patient at a time. Dental emergencies are the exception: call or message on WhatsApp and we will fit you in the same day wherever possible.

Will I be told the cost before treatment starts?

Yes. After the examination you get the diagnosis, the options and the cost of each in writing, before anything begins. If a treatment needs several visits, you will know how many and what each one covers.

Who will treat me?

Dr. Kshitija P Pervi, B.D.S (MUHS), Reg No. A-44613, treats every patient personally from diagnosis to follow-up.

Which treatment do I need?

That is what the first appointment is for. An examination and, where it helps, a digital X-ray show what is actually going on. You will see the X-ray yourself and hear the options in plain language before you decide anything.
