# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local patients (families, working adults, parents booking for kids) in the clinic's neighbourhood, mostly on mobile, arriving from the Google Business Profile or Instagram. Job to be done: judge trust in under a minute, see treatments offered, and book an appointment with the least friction (WhatsApp or a call, not email).

## Product Purpose

Single-page marketing site for Evara Dental Clinic, run by Dr. Kshitija P Pervi, B.D.S (MUHS), Reg No. A-44613. Success = an appointment request sent (WhatsApp form or tap-to-call) and the clinic feeling as warm and premium as its physical storefront.

## Brand Personality

Warm, precise, reassuring. The physical brand is the reference: backlit gold logo on a cream fascia, dark walnut pillar, bilingual Marathi + English signage, soft teal chairs inside. The site should feel like standing in front of that glowing storefront at dusk.

## Anti-references

- Blue-and-white template dental sites with toothy stock grins and pill-shaped gradient buttons.
- Corporate hospital-chain coldness (Apollo/Clove-style directory pages).
- Generic AI landing-page grammar: eyebrow labels over every section, identical icon-card grids, hero metrics.

## Design Principles

1. **The storefront is the palette.** Walnut, warm sand, lamplit gold, a restrained teal echo. Nothing the physical clinic doesn't already wear.
2. **Bilingual is brand, not translation.** The Devanagari name (एवारा डेंटल क्लिनिक) appears as a deliberate brand mark, not an afterthought.
3. **One tap to a human.** Every fold offers a route to WhatsApp or a call; the form composes a WhatsApp message, no backend.
4. **Credentials carry trust.** Real registration number, real qualification, real reviews (placeholders clearly marked until GBP data is supplied).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: 4.5:1 body contrast, visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives for all animation, semantic landmarks, form labels. Devanagari text uses a proper Devanagari typeface, never a fallback.
